Longer video playback.

The Max is rated six hours longer than a 17 Pro Max. The smaller 18 Pro is rated at up to 36 hours.

Those are the eSIM-only models. The Max figure is 45 hours of video playback. They pick up two extra hours by using the space a physical SIM would have taken.

Apple does not print a battery capacity. One afternoon on one phone is not the rating. There is no setting to turn this on. The number is video playback, not a promise about your day.
