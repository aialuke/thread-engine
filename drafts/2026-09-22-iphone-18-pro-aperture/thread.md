# iphone-18-pro-aperture

---

Matching up Apple's latest iPhone 18 Pro against the 17 Pro.

At first glance, is this just last year's phone?

✓ Same screen size, resolution, brightness & refresh rate.
✓ Same wireless MagSafe charging rate.
✓ Same front camera.
✓ Same IP68 rating

Four things are different from the 17 Pro. You decide if it's worth upgrading.
🧵

---

1. Iris lens that opens and closes.

Dark video gets more light, Night mode finishes faster, and a group stays sharper when the lens closes down.

The main lens has four openings. f/1.48, f/1.8, f/2.8, and f/4.0. Six blades. A 17 Pro main lens is fixed at f/1.78.

f/1.48 is the wide stop. Apple puts faster Night mode photos, and the line "around 50 percent better low-light," on that opening. The same opening is what Apple means by improved low-light video. The lens opens, and the frame rate can drop. Apple calls the sensor new and does not publish a size. That low-light line is the wider hole.

f/4.0 is the closed stop. More of a group or a room stays sharp. At f/1.48 the background softens a little, up close. It is a phone sensor.

⚠️ Main camera only. Ultra Wide stays 13 mm at f/2.2. Telephoto stays 100 mm at 4x, f/2.8.

Default already moves the blades. When a picture needs a specific opening, open Camera, swipe up from the modes, and select Lens Aperture. Apple's service steps name the lowest circle, f1.48, and the highest, f4.0.

Shutter speed, white balance, and a histogram sit in that same menu. VERIFY the histogram label. Use it when a face is blowing out.

Normal video from the main camera can take a Cinematic blur afterward, up to 4K Dolby Vision at 60 fps. The 17 Pro tops out at 30 fps. VERIFY the taps. Apple also lists smart focus tracking, which holds a subject even after they leave the frame.

Reference Image is an opt-in on the main camera. Settings, then Camera, then Reference Image. Capture is unavailable in the EU at launch. China mainland cannot capture or develop one.

---

2. 7-core GPU.

Games hold a higher frame rate, and a long session stays faster than on a 17 Pro.

The A20 Pro has a 7-core GPU with Neural Accelerators. The 17 Pro's GPU is 6-core.

Apple says graphics are up to 40 percent faster than the 17 Pro. Reviews that ran their own tests saw about the same jump. You notice it in a game. The frame rate holds up longer.

Apple also says sustained performance is up to 40 percent better than the 17 Pro, for a long session and for a larger on-device model.

---

3. Dual Neural Engine.

On-device AI work finishes sooner, and a heavier model has more room to stay on the phone.

The A20 Pro has two 16-core Neural Engines. The 17 Pro has one. Apple calls that double the AI processing.

This block does not draw the screen. It runs the models behind dictation, recognition, and Apple Intelligence. More of that work can stay on the phone and finish sooner.

A 17 Pro already gets Siri AI with iOS 27, including the on-screen awareness and the more adjustable voice. Those are not locked to this chip. A public AI test moved about 40 percent, not double. The benefit is headroom, not a new button.

---

3. Improved battery life.

The Max is rated six hours longer than a 17 Pro Max. The smaller 18 Pro is rated at up to 36 hours.

Those are the eSIM-only models. The Max figure is 45 hours of video playback. They pick up two extra hours by using the space a physical SIM would have taken.

Apple does not print a battery capacity. One afternoon on one phone is not the rating. There is no setting to turn this on. The number is video playback, not a promise about your day.

---

✓ A main lens that opens and closes. Graphics up to 40 percent faster. More room for on-device models. A longer video-playback rating, on the eSIM figures.

The screen, the wireless charge, the front camera, and the water rating match the 17 Pro. Those four are the difference.

Reply with 16, 17, or 18 Pro.

On a 17 Pro, you already get Siri AI with iOS 27. You do not get this lens, this GPU, or this battery rating.

If the phone is in your hand, shoot one frame at f/4.0 and the same frame at f/1.48. That pair is the camera change.
