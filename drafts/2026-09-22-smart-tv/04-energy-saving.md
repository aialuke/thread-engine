Setting 3: Energy saving Off

The film still looks dim after you leave Filmmaker on. People blame the panel. The TV is throttling brightness to save power.

Samsung 2025: Settings → All Settings → General & Privacy → Power and Energy Saving. Turn off Energy Saving Solution, Brightness Optimisation, Motion Lighting.

Samsung 2024: same three toggles, but the door is Settings → All Settings → Support → Device Care → General & Privacy → Power and Energy Saving.

LG 2022–2025: All Settings → General → Energy Saving → Energy Saving Step → Off.

LG 2026 (webOS 26): All Settings → System → Energy Saving → Energy Saving Step → Off.

Sony: Settings → Display & Sound → Picture → Light sensor / Ambient light sensor → Off. Newer firmware also has Eco Dashboard → Power saving.

Hisense VIDAA: do not leave Picture Mode on Energy Saving. Picture Mode Settings → Automatic Light Sensor → Off. Intelligent Mode Settings → AI Energy mode → Off, or it will throw you back into Energy Saving.
