Setting 7: Viewing information

This one is not the picture. It is the other default from setup day: the TV logging what is on the screen.

Samsung: open the Privacy Choices app, or Settings. Turn off Viewing Information Services. If you also see Customised Ads or Interest-Based Advertising, turn those off too. Cannot find the label? Skip it.

Sony / Hisense Google TV: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. Official Google line: you cannot turn ads off. Delete the ID. Ads still appear. They stop using that ID.

LG Live Plus is not on an official support page this session. Do not guess the menu. Skip.

This does not fix the soap opera. Cards 1–5 did that. This stops the set sending viewing signatures after you already opened Settings.
