# Tool-swap background

Read this when scheduling or pinning a PAID → FREE post, reading how the series did, or deciding how hard to push for an operator test. Drafting a post needs only `SKILL.md`.

## Why testing stays optional

The operator's rule: "I don't need to test something to research and understand it". Research from the vendors' own pages is enough, so a test is a value-add and never a gate.

A test still helps in one way. X's rewards program counts content "you have personally created" and not "aggregated summaries" ([X Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)). What makes a PAID → FREE post the operator's own is the choice of swaps and the judgement in the proof lines; a test or screenshot adds first-hand proof on top of that.

## Series operations

- **Cadence:** one PAID → FREE parent every other day, inside the account's cap of two originals a day, hours apart (the cap `/next` applies). Categories go in `queue/topics.yaml` order.
- **Pin:** pin the newest PAID → FREE post that's live, sourced and unboosted, with no link (A14). The operator pins on X.
- **Makers and boosting:** `voice/exit-zero.md` → Makers and → Boosting.

## How it's judged

Root only, on organic engagement and visits per organic impression at the 36–60 hour read (the loop's rules). Bookmarks aren't a ranking weight (A19); copy-link shares are (A9) but aren't visible (`queue/research-backlog.md` item 2). With the header and shape fixed, category can be the one variable in an experiment once experiments resume.
