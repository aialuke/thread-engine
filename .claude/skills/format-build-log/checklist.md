# Checklist: build-log

- [ ] The operator answered what ran, what came out, how, and what broke
- [ ] Real proof attached: screenshot, recording or output of the actual run
- [ ] CLAIMS.md row for every figure, operator-run rows with their artifact; no VERIFY
- [ ] Sentence 1 says what came out, concretely
- [ ] One honest line on what broke or what's next
- [ ] Ends on one real question naming a fork, not a request to reply, like or share
- [ ] Under 280 characters if possible, never over 600 (`scripts/formats.py`)
- [ ] At most one self-reply card
- [ ] voice/exit-zero.md banned phrases and farm tells cut
- [ ] APPROVED is absent. The operator approves by typing /approve <slug>
