## 1. What exactly is a qualified impression (viewer tier, "Home Timeline", 50% visibility, uniqueness)?

### Takeaway
X defines a qualified impression in one sentence: a unique impression from a Premium user (Basic, Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business) on the Home Timeline feed, where at least 50% of the post is visible. X says nothing official about For You versus Following, or about search, profile, notifications or conversation views. It never says "For You" or "Following" at all.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** "Qualified impressions are unique impressions from Premium users (subscribers to X Premium Basic, Premium, Premium+, or Premium Business) on the Home Timeline feed, where at least 50% of the post is visible." — [X Help Center, Original Content Rewards, Wayback 16 Sep 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards). The sentence is unchanged from the 8 Aug 2026 launch snapshot ([Wayback 8 Aug 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260808035902/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)).
- **[OFFICIAL]** "Eligible creators earn payouts based on qualified impressions generated by their original content." — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** The payout-standards list requires content to "Receive impressions from Premium accounts on the Home Timeline". — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** The legal terms word it differently: "The payment is based on impressions from Premium users with your content on X." They do not mention Home Timeline, 50% visibility or uniqueness, and they defer to the Help Center "for the most updated information". — [Original Content Rewards Program Terms §5, effective 7 Aug 2026](https://legal.x.com/en/original-content-rewards-terms.html)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Uniqueness: "Impressions from the same account counted more than once per post" are excluded. — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Tier facts that matter for "Premium" versus "verified":
  - The Basic tier ($3/month) does not include a checkmark.
  - "Premium: Includes all Basic features plus a checkmark ... access to apply for Original Content Rewards".
  - The blue check appears for Premium and Premium+. Premium Business gets gold, and Premium Organizations (governments and multilateral organisations) gets grey.
  — [X Help Center, About X Premium, Wayback 16 Sep 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150103/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Nikita Bier, 18 Jan 2026 (so under the prior program): "@sxtvik Wrong. Replies don't count anymore. Only home timeline impressions." — [x.com/nikitabier/status/2012988561834873165](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2012988561834873165) (search-indexed title, not opened)
- **[SECONDARY]** A Grok answer posted by a user on 8 Aug 2026 says a Home Timeline impression is a view "in someone's main Home feed (the default 'For You' or 'Following' tab…)". It counts "generally once at least part of it enters the viewport". This is an AI answer, not X policy, and "part of it" conflicts with X's 50% rule. — [@keepgoingAnnie, 8 Aug 2026](https://x.com/keepgoingAnnie/status/2085924236040732984) (search snippet)
- **[SECONDARY]** A search-engine summary of press coverage says an impression qualifies when a Premium user sees at least 50% of a post "either in the 'For you' tab or in 'Following'". I could not trace this to any X-owned text. — the search results that surfaced it included [News247Plus](https://www.news247plus.com/news/business/x-launches-original-content-rewards-the-program-that-promises-to-pay-better-to-creators-who-publish-their-own-content-56639) (not opened)
- **[SECONDARY]** AndroidHeadlines (8 Aug 2026) says views must come from "unique, paid Premium subscribers viewing posts on their Home Timeline with at least 50% visibility". It notes that X gives no detail on Following versus For You, quotes, threads or boosts. — [AndroidHeadlines, 8 Aug 2026](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/08/x-launches-original-content-rewards-program-creator-payouts.html)

### Inferences
- "Home Timeline" is X's name for the Home surface, and both the For You and Following tabs live under Home. On the plain wording, both tabs probably qualify. Search, profile, notifications, Explore, bookmarks, lists and the conversation (post-detail) view are not "the Home Timeline feed", so on the plain wording they probably do not count. X has not confirmed either reading.
- The viewer-tier wording differs between the two thresholds. Payouts count impressions from **Premium users, including Basic**. The 500K eligibility gate counts impressions from **"verified users"**. Basic has no checkmark, so if "verified" means checkmarked (the usual X usage), Basic viewers may count toward pay but not toward the 500K gate. X does not define "verified users" on this page. The legal terms say only "Premium users".
- Premium Organizations (grey check) is not in the help page's list of viewer tiers for qualified impressions. Whether a grey-check viewer's impression counts is not stated.
- Uniqueness is per viewer per post. Showing the same post to the same Premium viewer again adds nothing, so reach to new Premium viewers is what moves the number.

### Gaps
- X has no official statement on the For You versus Following split, or on whether a post shown inside a Home Timeline conversation module (root plus inline reply) counts.
- It is unconfirmed whether the 500K eligibility count applies the same 50% visibility and uniqueness rules as payout "qualified impressions". The help page defines only the latter.
- X does not define "verified users" or "verified followers" for this program.
- I could not read the @XCreators launch article ([x.com/XCreators/status/2085835082166653393](https://x.com/XCreators/status/2085835082166653393), posted 7 Aug 2026 21:06 UTC). x.com was blocked and the Grok balance was exhausted, so it may contain wording beyond the help page. Press quotes from it match the help page.

## 2. What is excluded (replies, quotes, reposts, threads, boosted/promoted, fraudulent)?

### Takeaway
X officially excludes the following:
- repeat impressions from the same account on the same post
- paid, promoted or artificially generated impressions
- fraudulent impressions
- replies, from the eligibility count
- boosted impressions, from payment calculations (stated in the legal terms)
- posts that are not original, which "won't generate qualified impressions"
- posts that carry a helpful Community Note, are misleading, automated, and so on (these are ineligible)

X says nothing explicit about quote posts, reposts, or the later posts of a thread.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** Excluded from qualified impressions: "Impressions from the same account counted more than once per post; Paid, promoted, or artificially generated impressions; Fraudulent impressions". — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Eligibility: "at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days (impressions on replies are excluded)". — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Boosted: "You agree and acknowledge that your content is eligible to be boosted under the terms of the Boosted Program and that any impressions as a result of your content being boosted are excluded in the calculation of any payments under the Program." — [Terms §5, effective 7 Aug 2026](https://legal.x.com/en/original-content-rewards-terms.html)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Artificial inflation: "If you are found to artificially inflate the number of views on your posts through promoted post, manual, automated, or other means, or manipulate the platform to alter the views on your posts, you will forfeit any amount due to you and you will be terminated from the Program." X may also "withhold ... from our calculation any activity that we believe were due to any breach of the X User Agreement". — [Terms §4–5](https://legal.x.com/en/original-content-rewards-terms.html)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Non-original content: "If the primary value comes from someone else's work and you've done little to transform or add to it, it won't generate qualified impressions under the program." — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Content is ineligible if any of the following is true:
  - It is copied or substantially reproduced.
  - It is downloaded and reuploaded (unless you are the author).
  - It is "inappropriate, sexually explicit, or potentially harmful".
  - It is "exclusively focused on monetization coaching, monetization discussion, or maximizing payouts".
  - It "Was created or posted using automated means".
  - It "Contains disinformation or misleading content".
  - It "Has a helpful Community Note".
  - It violates the Community Guidelines, Terms of Service or Copyright Policy.

  — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Replies were already out under the prior program. Nikita Bier said on 18 Jan 2026: "Replies don't count anymore. Only home timeline impressions." — [x.com/nikitabier/status/2012988561834873165](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2012988561834873165) (search-indexed title)
- **[OFFICIAL / X-hosted AI summary]** An X Trending/Explore story on 14 Aug 2026 was titled "X Cuts Replies from Monetization Impressions to Curb Spam Bots". Its body could not be fetched (402). The search summary says the reason given was that spam bots were replying to hit the threshold. — [x.com/i/trending/2088308826693115991](https://x.com/i/trending/2088308826693115991). These X "trending" pages are machine-written summaries, not policy.
- **[OFFICIAL, PRIOR context]** Nikita Bier, 23 May 2026, on reuploads: "we have identified a number of large accounts that have been programmatically reuploading content from smaller accounts to game the revenue share program ... We are now identifying these posts and allocating the impr[essions]…" to the original author. — [x.com/nikitabier/status/2058020001823793265](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2058020001823793265) (snippet truncated by the search index). Also on 25 May 2026: "we cannot tolerate big accounts intentionally using their larger following to hijack impressions hours after another user posts something." — [x.com/nikitabier/status/2058810803625881883](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2058810803625881883)
- **[SECONDARY]** AndroidHeadlines summarises the exclusions as "duplicate, fraudulent, paid, or promoted impressions" plus replies. — [AndroidHeadlines, 8 Aug 2026](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/08/x-launches-original-content-rewards-program-creator-payouts.html). Complex adds "Replies and artificially generated views excluded from impression counts". — [Complex, 9 Aug 2026](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/markelibert/x-original-content-rewards-program-creator-payouts)

### Inferences
- **Threads.** The second and later posts of a thread are technically replies to your own root. On the plain reading of "impressions on replies are excluded", only the root post's Home Timeline impressions count. For a multi-card thread, the root carries all of the countable impressions, and a reach-heavy root matters more than card count.
- **Quote posts** are standalone posts, not replies, so their Home Timeline impressions should count when the quote adds real commentary. X calls commentary "the heart of X". A quote with minimal added value risks the "primary value comes from someone else's work" exclusion. X has not confirmed this.
- **Reposts of your post.** When someone reposts your post, it shows in their followers' Home Timelines as your post. X has not said whether that counts as an impression on your post. Bier's May 2026 work allocating impressions to original authors suggests X attributes by original author.
- **Boosted posts.** Paying to boost a post adds nothing to payable impressions. Using promotion to inflate views is grounds for forfeiture and termination under §5. The legal terms exclude boosted impressions only from "payments". Whether boosted impressions also count toward the 500K eligibility gate is not stated. The help page's blanket exclusion of "Paid, promoted" impressions suggests not.

### Gaps
- There is no X-owned statement on quote posts, reposts, or self-reply thread cards specifically.
- It is unknown whether an ineligible post (for example one with a helpful Community Note) still adds to the 500K eligibility count, or only drops out of payouts.
- The operator reports the Creator Studio eligibility screen text "Does not include replies". I could not observe the screen, because signing in was out of scope. It matches the help-page wording.

## 3. Eligibility: 500 verified followers, 500K in 90 days, how progress is shown, when counting started

### Takeaway
To apply you need all of the following at the time of application:
- a supported country, age 18 or over, and a Personal or Business account in good standing
- an active Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business subscription (not Basic)
- 500 or more "verified followers"
- 500,000 or more Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days, excluding replies
- active original posting

Progress shows in Creator Studio → Original Content Rewards. X publishes no refresh cadence or delay, and no counting start date. Once you are admitted, the legal terms say falling below the impressions threshold does not remove you.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** The full list, "at the time of application":
  - "Location: Be based in a country where the Original Content Rewards Program is available". The list covers about 120 countries, including Australia, the US, the UK and Canada.
  - "Age: Be 18+".
  - "Account standing: ... no history of repeatedly violating our Monetization Guidelines or Terms of Service".
  - "Account type: Have a Personal or Business Account. Accounts belonging to political or government organizations are not eligible."
  - "Premium subscription: Hold an active X Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business subscription".
  - "Impressions: Have at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days (impressions on replies are excluded)".
  - "Followers: Have at least 500 verified followers".
  - "Original content: Actively post original content as defined in this policy".

  — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)

If the appeal is unsuccessful, you can reapply after 42 days". ### Inferences
- "In the last 90 days" reads as a rolling window, so old impressions age out daily. X does not say so explicitly.
- X was already measuring "Verified Home Timeline impressions" and excluding replies under Revenue Sharing, from at least Jan 2026 (the Bier post and the 3 Aug help page). The 90-day window at launch therefore most likely looked back over pre-launch activity rather than starting from zero on 7 Aug or 8 Sep. This is unconfirmed.
- "Verified followers" most likely means followers with a checkmark: blue (Premium or Premium+), gold (Business) and possibly grey (Organizations). Basic subscribers have no check and probably do not count. X does not define the term for this program.
- The help page excludes "political or government organizations". The legal terms go further and bar individuals: "You may not participate in the Program if you work for a government institution, are an elected official, are a political party, or engaged in any election-related activity." ([Terms §4](https://legal.x.com/en/original-content-rewards-terms.html)) State-affiliated media are barred separately. None of this bars political content from ordinary creators, but the Monetization Standards list "exploitation of controversial political or social issues" as restricted "Sensitive Content".
- For a small creator, 500K is an entry gate. Under the legal terms you are not removed if you later drop below it.

### Gaps
- X publishes no refresh cadence or processing delay for the Creator Studio progress bar or its numbers. I found no official statement.
- X states no counting start date. It has not said whether pre-launch impressions count.
- There is no official definition of "verified follower", and in particular nothing on whether gold, grey or Basic followers count.

## 4. Originality standard, engagement bait, Community Notes, penalties, political/government exclusion

### Takeaway
Original means content you personally created that reflects your voice or expertise. Commentary that adds genuine perspective counts. Copied, minimally modified, aggregated or cross-platform-reposted content does not. Repeatedly asking users to like, reply, bookmark, follow or repost is a breach that can remove you. A helpful Community Note makes a post ineligible. Enforcement ranges from temporary to permanent removal.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** "Original content is content you have personally created – written, filmed, designed, or produced – that reflects your own voice, perspective, or expertise." It goes on: "Commentary is at the heart of X ... breaking down a news story, sharing your take on a cultural moment, or adding context others don't have, counts as original". — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Not original:
  - "Copied content ... This includes content that is downloaded from X or other platforms and then reuploaded"
  - "Minimally modified content ... changing a word or two in a post, adding a filter, adjusting video speed, or placing a text overlay"
  - "Aggregated content: Posts, videos, or images that primarily compile or combine content from other creators or sources without adding substantial new perspective or framing"
  - "Cross-platform reposts: Content taken from other platforms and reposted by someone other than the original creator"

  — [X Help Center, 16 Sep 2026 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Originality is separate from IP: "Content may qualify as original under this program and still violate our intellectual property rules." — [X Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Engagement solicitation is a continuous-eligibility breach: "Do not solicit engagements: repeatedly instructing users to engage with posts, such as asking to like, reply, bookmark, follow, or repost." Bots and automated tools that "artificially generate likes, follows, views, comments, or shares" are banned, as is tampering with "recommendation algorithms". — [X Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Penalties: "you will receive a notification. Depending on the severity of the violation, your account may be temporarily or permanently removed ... You may appeal." X "reserves the right to ... take enforcement action for conduct that undermines the integrity ... even if such conduct is not specifically listed above." Posting AI-generated armed-conflict video without an AI disclosure brings a 90-day program suspension, and a repeat brings a permanent payment suspension (effective 3 Mar 2026). — [X Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Community Notes: content is ineligible if it "Has a helpful Community Note" or "Contains disinformation or misleading content". — [X Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Automation: content is ineligible if it "Was created or posted using automated means". — [X Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Political and government: help page, "Accounts belonging to political or government organizations are not eligible"; legal terms §4, government-institution workers, elected officials, political parties and anyone "engaged in any election-related activity" may not participate. — [X Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20260916150107/https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards); [Terms §4](https://legal.x.com/en/original-content-rewards-terms.html)
- **[OFFICIAL, PRIOR]** Nikita Bier, 11 Apr 2026: "All aggregators had their payouts reduced to 60% this cycle. We will add another 20% deduction in the next cycle. It became abundantly clear: flooding the timeline with 100 stolen reposts and clickbait everyday crowded-out real creators and hurt new author growth." — [x.com/nikitabier/status/2043045929750794399](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2043045929750794399) (search-indexed title)
- **[SECONDARY reporting of OFFICIAL, PRIOR]** Social Media Today (12 Apr 2026) reports "permanent deductions coming for habitual users of clickbait phrases like 'BREAKING'" and quotes Bier: "This means rewarding the effort it takes to produce something, not just the poster who helped it travel furthest." — [Social Media Today, 12 Apr 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/x-boosts-incentives-for-original-content-creators/817271/). NBC quotes Bier: "X will never infringe on speech or reach—but we will not compensate for manipulation of the program." — [NBC News, 12 Apr 2026](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/x-slashes-aggregator-payouts-boost-original-creators-rcna324820)
- **[SECONDARY reporting of OFFICIAL]** TechCrunch quotes X's Allegra Jacchia (her title was not captured). - **[SECONDARY]** Complex describes the engagement-bait rule as posts encouraging likes, reposts, replies, bookmarks or follows "solely to increase engagement". This wording may come from the @XCreators article, which I could not verify. — [Complex, 9 Aug 2026](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/markelibert/x-original-content-rewards-program-creator-payouts)

### Inferences
- End-of-post calls to action ("follow for more", "bookmark this", "reply with…") are the exact behaviour X names. The word "repeatedly" suggests a pattern test rather than a single post, but X does not define a threshold.
- "Created or posted using automated means" is not defined. AI-drafted text that a human posts by hand is not clearly covered, but there is room for risk: X also bans "automated tools" and wants "your own voice". Scheduled or bot posting is plainly out.
- The Community Note rule applies per post. One bad fact costs that post's eligibility, and repeated misleading content risks the account.
- Posts "exclusively focused on monetization coaching ... or maximizing payouts" are ineligible. Content about this program itself earns nothing.

### Gaps
- There is no official definition of "repeatedly" for solicitation, of "automated means", or of how "aggregated" is judged (human review or classifier).
- It is not stated whether a Community Note added after a post has accrued impressions removes those impressions retroactively.

## 5. How payouts relate to qualified impressions (formula, RPM) and what X says about earning more

### Takeaway
X publishes no RPM, rate or formula. It says only that payouts are "based on qualified impressions generated by their original content" (help page) or "based on impressions from Premium users" (legal terms). Payouts run every two weeks, with a $30 minimum. X reserves the right to change the calculation at any time. X's only official advice on earning more is to make net new, original content: commentary, analysis, your own video and voice.

### Inferences
- Under the program's own definitions, the levers that raise qualified impressions are:
  - more unique Premium viewers per post (new reach, not repeat views)
  - reaching them on the Home Timeline, not via profile or search
  - posts that hold at least 50% on screen long enough to register
  - root posts rather than replies
  - staying clear of Community Notes, bait and non-original material, since any of these zeroes a post
- Earlier Revenue Sharing rules weighted viewer tier (Premium+ above Basic), content format and region. The new pages do not repeat this weighting, and X does not say it was dropped. Treat per-impression value as variable and undisclosed.

### Gaps
- X has published no RPM, per-impression rate, revenue-pool size or weighting formula for Original Content Rewards.
- It is not stated whether viewer tier, region or format weighting carries over from Revenue Sharing.
- I found no official X "how to earn more" guidance beyond the originality and commentary guidance above. The @XCreators launch article could not be read and may contain some.
