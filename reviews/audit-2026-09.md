## Decisions for you

### 1. Who the account is for

The people who follow are **AI builders**, and they arrived through networking in the builder community. The `main`-lane posts in `audience.md` (three device-settings threads and the iPhone comparison) produced **0 profile visits across 4 posts**. The creator-tool swap reached almost nobody organically (99 impressions).

- **Recommendation:** position the account as **an Australian builder making an AI idea-to-video pipeline, sharing the tools and lessons**. That fits the bio, the followers you already have, and what earns visits. "Free tools that hold up" and AI tool comparisons fit this. Device-settings threads become occasional, not the core.
- **Then:**
  - rewrite `reference/audience.md` for builders and creators who use AI;
  - keep the bio (it already fits);
  - pin a post that shows the pipeline or a lesson from building it, with no link (A14).
- **Unpin the tool-swap either way.** Its reach was 95% paid, and its "No watermark" line has no source (truth budget).
- **The alternative:** keep the device-tips audience. Then the bio and pin have to change to match, and networking with builders stops feeding it. The data so far gives that direction no support, though three threads posted at around 30 followers is a thin test.

### 2. Replies

Politics and news replies bring the most reach (6,680 impressions, 15 visits), but the audience they reach isn't the one that follows: 1 follower of 36 has a politics or news bio. Three things carry real risk (`reference/x-algorithm.md`):
- **The same reply was pasted 12 times in 3 minutes** ("Would love to connect and see what others are creating 👋"), and "👋" alone 6 times. A13: duplicate reply text is clustered and can be labelled `COPYPASTA_SPAM`.
- **68 replies in one 24-hour stretch.** A12: the reply spam scorer sees the replier's reply count for the last 24 hours. No threshold is documented, so this is a risk to watch, not a proven penalty.
- **Politics invites reports, mutes and blocks**, the heaviest negative weights in A9 (report −234).

**Recommendation:**
- Never repeat a reply's text.
- Keep reply volume to what you'd write by hand.
- Put political and news replies on `awakenluke`.
- On @exitzerocode, reply in the builder and AI community with specific replies (name their project, ask a real question). The reply that did that best got 2 profile visits from 11 impressions.

### 3. Networking posts

The "Drop a hi" post produced **20 of the account's 58 profile visits**, and networking replies turn views into visits at 10× the rate of other replies (23.7 vs 2.3 per 1,000). It's also what `voice/exit-zero.md` calls a farm tell, and a third of your followers show a follow-back pattern, which tends to mean follows that don't turn into engagement.

**Recommendation:** keep networking, but make it personal. Specific replies to builders' progress posts, yes. Generic "👋", pasted lines and "drop a hi" posts, no. If you want an occasional connect post, it becomes an `other`-lane post at most 1 in 15, and the voice guide says so. Your call.

### 4. Boosting

The Boost on the tool-swap bought about 2,006 impressions. They produced about 4 profile visits (your analytics screen showed 5 visits in total against 1 organic) and 0 extra likes. The profile those visitors landed on didn't match the post.

**Recommendation:** don't boost until the profile and pin match the audience from decision 1. Never boost a post that's part of an experiment.

## Appendix

**The loop's limits.**
- Before this audit it saw 7 of the account's 157 items.
- It scored views, which include paid reach. That's fixed now: views and root replies can no longer be an experiment's measure (commit `deddbfa`).
- Its bar was 1.5× the cohort median. The median profile visits is 0 in every group, so every post would have passed. A zero median is now refused.
- Control posts aren't scored.
- **Experiments are paused** until two weeks of organic data exist.

**Data gaps.**
- Per-post follows and shares are Enterprise-only in the API (`reference/x-api.md` P11). Follows will be credited by matching new followers to who replied to us and who we replied to, as a lower bound.
- Your analytics CSV may have exact per-post follows. Not checked yet.
- Production time isn't recorded.
