# UI review: seven adversarial reviewers, 24 Sep 2026

Seven Claude reviewers read the mock (https://claude.ai/artifact/8zaBcxWAknJaXWkReQQPqg, source in the session scratchpad), the decision log (`reviews/ui-direction.md`, D1–D23) and the repo. Each had one angle:

1. the operator at 06:00;
2. a newcomer the app is shared with;
3. rules and safety;
4. whether each element can be built;
5. accessibility;
6. consistency with the decisions;
7. product strategy.

Claude checked the main claims against the files before merging them. Duplicates are merged. Most severe first within each part.

## A. Design flaws in the mock (to fix before building)

1. **Posting has no unhappy paths.**
   - **Finding the post:** it always succeeds. There's no "not found yet", no "found with differences", and no check for a double post.
   - **Copying:** a failed copy still says "It's on your clipboard", and the copy button disappears. Get ready also claims it already put the post on the clipboard.
   - **Errors and questions:** the gate's refusals and a failed fact-check have no screen. The working-state line has no "needs you" or error state. Several commands stop to ask a question (`/posted` minutes, `/results` eligibility numbers, `/next` accept), and nowhere is designed for that.
   - **Shout-out:** the 10–20 minute window never closes. "Mark as done without it" only appears after copying the shout-out, and runs the same search as posting it.
2. **Time is hard-coded.**
   - On the posting morning, the header still says "Tomorrow, 06:00", and a late post's countdown reads "tomorrow".
   - Approving at 21:30 puts "Get ready" under the thumb, and one tap reaches "Posting now".
   - **Needs:** relative times, a late state inside the 05:00–07:00 slot, and a check when posting is more than about 30 minutes early.
3. **Approval isn't tied to the real cards.**
   - The preview and "329 of 600" are hard-coded.
   - "Edit a card" only shows a warning, and "cards locked" isn't true.
   - **Needs:** approval state computed from the cards' digest on every view (the duplicate above proves it), an editor for changes before and after approval, and "changed since approval" when that's true.
4. **Today puts "Worth joining" rows under the "3 people are waiting" banner.** Waiting cards have no Answered or Dismiss. The badge would show 0, and the banner would say "1 people".
5. **Numbers disagree.**
   - The headline says every visit came from one post, while the tiles include the boosted post: 21 visits, 2,329 views.
   - "Yesterday: 68 replies", but the chart shows 9 that day.
   - Follows: 13 + 4 ≠ 19, because 2 came from thread cards. Visits: 58 against 54.
   - Posts lists 6 posts; Results lists 11.
   - "Two days after" is actually four.
   - Home Wi-Fi and MacBook battery show 0 follows, but the ledger has 1 each.
   - "Snapshot ran 15:31. Every read is in.": the last run was 20:00, and 6 posts are still under 36 hours old.
6. **Cortex oversteps.**
   - It offers one-tap action buttons whose label and effect the model writes ("Queue it for 8 Oct"). That contradicts "Cortex suggests; only you apply", and a reply it reads could inject one.
   - `/apply` and `/undo-rule` have no buttons (D2).
   - "It's set the way the evidence says" isn't true while there are 0 lessons.
   - "Post length up to 600" is a gate limit shown as a tunable weight.
   - The guardrail wording is weaker than AGENTS.md, and the guardrails aren't a separate card (D11).
7. **Unsourced or duplicated guidance.**
   - The same talking point ("read-only keys, and a hook on the one file only you can create") is suggested for two conversations (A13).
   - Cortex answers claim the fold sits at about 280, and that connect posts are ruled out (voice allows 1 in 15).
8. **"Open your post on X" links to the profile, not the post.** Step 1 has no link into X at all.
9. **Colour meaning drifts from D20.** Amber is used for the fold line, the example lesson, "Paused", and "If someone pushes back". Meanwhile the one real "needs you" item, people waiting, is blue.
10. **Loose ends against the decisions.**
    - "Searching X for questions in your lane" breaks D19.
    - "Learned 14 Oct".
    - "Get ready" is the button for the Post step, and an unused "Researching" stage name remains (D15).
    - D14 is half built: there's no "Ask Cortex" on a proposal or a waiting reply, and on the Mac it leaves the page.
    - The theme falls back to light rather than following the device (D23).
    - Four charts have no table, and "Followers over time" is a line (D22).
    - No amber dot on the profile button (D8).
    - The Mac's "Approve the cards on the right" button does nothing.
    - All commands share one working state, so one silently cancels another.
    - A stale "About:" chip.

## B. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

1. **Hold to approve works only with a pointer.** Keyboard, Mac VoiceOver and Switch Control users can't approve at all. It needs a keyboard hold and an accessible confirm, without turning it into a plain tap (D13).
2. **The sheets aren't dialogs.** There's no focus move, no focus trap, no Escape, and no modal semantics.
3. **Focus and announcements.** Focus is lost when a pressed button disappears. Finished work isn't announced. The chat isn't a log.
4. **Contrast failures (computed):**

   | Element | Mode | Ratio |
   |---|---|---|
   | Replies badge | dark | 4.43 |
   | Hold label | dark | 4.09 |
   | Off-topic green | light | 2.82 |
   | Dimmed upcoming steps | light | 3.44 |
   | Shimmer peak | light | 1.76 |
   | Selected segment | both | 1.28 / 1.15 |
   | Mac active nav | both | 1.17 |
   | Active iPhone tab | dark | 2.93 |
   | "Ask Cortex…" | dark | 4.45 |

   Suggested replacements:
   - `--chart-off` light: `#15936A`
   - dark badge background: `#4466E0`
   - dark hold fill: `--blue`
   - `--shimmer-hi` light: `#7A7466`
   - dark `--faint`: `#99938A`
5. **Colour alone carries state.** On and off topic, the stepper, the active tab and the selected segment differ only by colour.
6. **Small or truncated text.**
   - 41 font sizes are under 12px.
   - Sizes are fixed px, so iOS Dynamic Type has no effect.
   - Titles are truncated in the iPhone chart.
   - "99*" has no key.
7. **Touch targets.** Several are under Apple's 44pt: pills 34, chips 38, the × 32, EZ and Send 40. All pass WCAG's 24px minimum.
8. **Already good:** reduced motion is covered everywhere, links are 44px, and icon buttons are labelled.

## C. Build realities (feed the build plan)

1. **Nothing can run from the iPhone today.**
   - The keys live in the Mac's Keychain, the only job is launchd at 20:00, copying uses `pbcopy` (the Mac's clipboard), and the commands are skills inside CLI sessions.
   - **Needs:** a Mac-side service running headless agents with the project's hooks, secure remote access, the phone's own clipboard, and a "Mac unreachable" health state.
2. **The Approve button's safety (reopens D13).**
   - Agent sessions on this Mac have computer-use and browser tools that can press and hold a button on screen, or call its endpoint directly. The hold stops accidents but doesn't prove a person pressed it.
   - The rules say only the operator approves, and the loop may never change that gate.
   - **Options:**
     - Keep typed `/approve` as the only real gate until solved.
     - Have approvals signed by Touch ID / Face ID (passkey or Secure Enclave), with the hold on top.
3. **"Waiting for you" needs more than `referenced_tweets`.**
   - The operator's own replies only reach the ledger at 36 hours.
   - The text of replies to the operator isn't stored anywhere tracked (keep it private, in `loop/followers/`).
   - Greetings need sorting from replies with substance.
   - **Cost:** a live read on open is about $0.06–0.10 for the first read each UTC day.
4. **Live features have no machinery.** Live replies, the 10-minute nudge and shout-out detection need a poller and push. On iPhone, web push only works for a home-screen app with permission. Finding the post itself is feasible: a timeline read, about $0.001–0.01.
5. **"Worth joining" isn't stored.** It's `/next` chat output, at up to about $0.15 of X API per search. Store it and show "found 05:40".
6. **Health and spend.** Spend is an incomplete estimate (the `x_read`, CLI and Grok costs aren't logged). "Keys working" only checks the keys exist.
7. **The Plan button rerunning the snapshot double-charges** across the UTC day boundary (10:00 Brisbane). Follower reads grow with followers.
8. **Weights aren't stored anywhere.** They're prose in the skills. A structured file would be needed if the UI shows them.
9. **Working-state texts don't match the real commands.** Drafting takes minutes, not 7 seconds, and "Booking the 36–60 hour read" isn't a real step.
10. **Privacy.** Real handles, links and paraphrased replies from other people sit in an artifact meant to be shared. Use made-up data in anything shared, keep third-party content local, and mark each suggested post "checked against X".

## D. Strategy challenges (for the operator to decide)

The product strategist's case: the UI runs the PAID → FREE pipeline and shows numbers well, but under-serves what grows the account.

1. **No way to make first-hand content.** Research ranks a video of the pipeline and build logs as the top levers, and X's originality rules favour first-hand posts. Nothing in the UI captures a screenshot, recording or voice note and turns it into a build-log or tool-verdict draft. Proposal: a Capture inbox, and reader questions shown as post ideas.
2. **Approval as a one-minute formality.** "About a minute" and "Editing clears the approval" push toward approving as-is, and the loop gets no edit signal (the weekly review found none). Proposal: free edits before approval, a choice of hooks, and a "my take" line.
3. **Results leads with views.** Reply views don't count toward rewards, and the goal numbers sit in the third tab. Proposal: Growth first, progress on Today, posts sorted by follows.
4. **Replies measure volume, not relationships.** Mutual follows (A6, A10) appear nowhere. Proposal: a small Builders list with a mutuals count; drop the replies-sent chart.
5. **The flow ends about 20 minutes after posting,** but the first like and first hour decide reach (A6). Proposal: a one-hour live card, recording time to first like.
6. **Cortex is decoration until experiments run (from about 8 Oct),** and the queued experiments skip the biggest levers. Proposal: defer the chat; test build-log against PAID → FREE first.
7. **The on-topic chart invites the wrong lesson.** All 20 visits came from one retired connect post.
8. **Polish before growth.** Proposal: defer dark mode, the Mac app, sharing and table views, and build phone-first and minimal.

The strategist's "first version": phone only; Today with progress; draft review with free edits, a hook choice, a "my take" line and the share question; posting plus a first-hour card; Replies with mutuals; a Capture inbox; one Results list sorted by follows. Defer Cortex, the Mac app, dark mode and table views.
