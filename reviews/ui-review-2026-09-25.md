## 6. Outside the mock

`ledger/SUMMARY.md` shows macbook-battery at 151 organic views against 149 total. Both numbers are real reads, about six hours apart. `render()` in `scripts/loop.py` (about lines 1124–1136) takes Views from the post's snapshot and Organic from the later activity read. The same happens on connect-pin (849 against 856) and iphone-18-pro-aperture (464 against 509). The mock isn't affected: its figures come from single reads. Fix `render()` to take both numbers from the same read, in a loop session.
