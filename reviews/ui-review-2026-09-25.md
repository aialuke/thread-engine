# UI review: five code reviewers, 25 Sep 2026

The second full review of the Thread Engine mock (https://claude.ai/artifact/8zaBcxWAknJaXWkReQQPqg, published version `1790291209-3f62`). Five reviewers read the mock's source in parallel; none used a browser, so layout, clipping and real behaviour are left to the operator's own click-through in Chrome. The mock was not changed.

| Reviewer | Checked | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Regression | Every item in `ui-review-2026-09-24.md` | about 24 landed, 11 partial, 4 not landed or not checkable |
| Decisions | D1–D31 on iPhone, Mac and dark | 27 match, 3 deviate, none missing, no rejected option visible |
| Wording | Every visible sentence against `voice/exit-zero.md`, the gate and the truth budget | 3 must fix, 3 should fix, 1 nit |
| Logic | The interaction code in full | 1 blocker, 8 major, 11 minor |
| Data | About 46 numbers recomputed from `ledger/`, `loop/`, the weekly review and the drafts | 40 match, 4 mismatch, 1 unsourced, 1 borderline |

Main and Mac run the same script and the same data, so unless a row says otherwise a finding applies to both. Findings that more than one reviewer raised appear once, with every reviewer named.

## 1. Blocker

| # | Finding | Found by | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **An edited card can be posted without approving it again.** `saveEdit` correctly drops the stage from 3 to 2, but `doneReady` (Main script line 1069) sets stage 4 unconditionally when the ~5 s "Start posting" line ends. Start posting, edit and save a card before the line ends, and the app says the final checks passed and copies unapproved text. Checked by hand. | Logic B1 | `doneReady` proceeds only if the stage is still 3 and the card digest still matches the approval; otherwise show "You changed a card after approving it". Disable editing while checks run. |

## 2. Must fix: safety, truth, broken promises

| # | Finding | Found by | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Hold-to-approve can approve without a real hold.** No `pointercancel`, `lostpointercapture` or `blur` handling. An iOS gesture cancel, or Tab while holding Space, approves after 1 s; one synthetic pointerdown or keydown does too, which matters for D24. | Logic M1 | Cancel the hold on cancel, blur, leave and visibility change; require an uninterrupted trusted press for the full duration. |
| 3 | **The Builders card gets fact A10 backwards.** "When a mutual replies to your post, X weighs it four times as much as a stranger's reply." `reference/x-algorithm.md` A10: the weight on *the viewer replying to a mutual's original* rises from 5 to 20, "not replies from mutuals". Checked by hand. | Wording | "Mutuals see your posts ranked higher: X weighs their chance of replying at 20 instead of 5 (A10)." |
| 4 | **"Plus 37 greetings and connect requests this week" has no source.** No repo file gives 37 (the only count is 25 greetings on one post), and unlike the other sample tiles it has no "example" tag. Checked by hand. | Wording, Data | Compute it from `ledger/activity/`, or tag it "example". |
| 5 | **The Weights panel says "Set by you · 24 Sep" for weights nothing has set.** `loop/state.json` has no lessons and no rules; nothing has been through `/apply`. | Data | Label the panel as the starting rules from the format skills, or tag it "example". |
| 6 | **The final checks pass any edit:** over 600 characters, no PAID → FREE header, "reply with", or an empty card. | Logic M7 | Mirror the gate's refusals (`scripts/post_thread.py`) in the mock's check, or show a refused state for these cases. |
| 7 | **A failed shout-out copy still says "Copied".** The clipboard then still holds card 1, so pasting would post the list again as a reply. The shout-out is recorded about 5 s after the copy tap, before it is pasted. | Logic M8 | Show the copy-failed card; record only when the shout-out is found on X (D17) or on Done. |
| 8 | **"Remind me at 05:50" opens Settings, where the reminder shows Off and can't be switched on.** | Logic M6 | Make the reminder a working toggle, or drop the button. |

## 3. Major: flows and decisions

| # | Finding | Found by | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **A missed posting time is a dead end, and the mock is already past it.** The slot is hard-coded to 25 Sep 06:00 Brisbane. "Pick a new time" proposes a different post and never moves this one, while Start posting stays live. From 26 Sep the headline reads "Tomorrow's post" for a past slot. Hard-coded 06:00 text elsewhere too (logic m8). | Logic M2, m8 | Compute the slot relative to now; "Pick a new time" reschedules this post. |
| 10 | **No way back once posting starts:** no Not now, edit or un-approve at stage 4. | Logic M3 | Add Not now (back to stage 3) and Withdraw approval (back to 2). |
| 11 | **The first-hour card never closes (D29).** Its close time is computed but never compared with the clock. | Logic M4 | Close it at posted time + 60 min. |
| 12 | **The Wait timer (D17) starts when the app finds the post, not at the real posting time,** and counts down per tick, so it drifts when the phone throttles timers. No 10-minute notification. | Logic M5 | Derive remaining time from the post's `created_at` and `Date.now()` each tick; show the notification at 10 minutes. |
| 13 | **Commands that stop to ask a question have no UI:** `/posted` minutes, `/results` eligibility numbers, `/next` accept. | Regression A1e | A "needs you" state in the working line with the question and answer buttons. |
| 14 | **The working line has no general error or needs-you state.** Only ready, copy and find got their own error cards; `/next`, `/draft-thread` and `/results` failures have nowhere to show. | Regression A1d | One error state and one needs-you state in `Thinking.dc.html`, used by every command. |
| 15 | **D22: the Posts tab has no table view.** "Show as table" only switches Growth and Replies. | Decisions | Add a table for the on/off-topic strip. |
| 16 | **D27: posts aren't sorted by follows, then visits, then views.** The list is in declaration order. | Decisions | Sort in `rposts`. |
| 17 | **The 600-character cap and "result first" are shown as rules for every format, and the cap is still under Weights.** The gate exempts comparisons from the cap; "result first" is the settings format's rule (comparison opens on both products). Yesterday's review asked for the cap to move to Guardrails. | Data, Regression A6d | Say which formats each applies to; move the cap to Guardrails. |

## 5. Minor

From the logic audit: leaving a screen mid-task leaves `busy` stuck and quietly disables buttons (m3); the capture "Queued" status is keyed by list index and moves to the wrong item (m4); the Posted list is sorted by weekday name (m5); "Read and approve" in the queue opens the wrong post (m6); "I've posted it" is offered after the copy failed (m7); the character counter doesn't count a link as 23 or joined emoji as X does (m9); "Answered" and "Not answering" do the same thing and can't be undone (m10); literal `{{ true }}` holes may leave the 30/90-day and example Apply/Undo buttons enabled (m11, uncertain).

From the decisions check: D26's hook picker isn't demonstrated, because the only editable draft is a tool-swap with a fixed header. Not a contradiction.

## 6. Checked and fine

- No banned phrase or farm tell anywhere. The two sample cards pass every gate refusal and match `drafts/2026-09-24-paid-free-developer/` word for word, every figure traced to its `CLAIMS.md`.
- "Lane" never appears on screen (D19). D12, D16 and D18 wording is exact on both devices.
- Dark mode is designed, not inverted (D21); the toggle sits where D23 says.
- 40 of about 46 numbers match the repo, including 27 of 500 followers, 337 of 500,000 impressions, 2,230 views / 20 visits / 15 follows, the on-topic share 5 of 11, the 68-reply peak, dates, weekdays and Brisbane times.
- From yesterday's review: time handling, approval tied to the card digest, Today's order, reconciled numbers, the marked Cortex example, the Guardrails split, amber only for "needs you", keyboard hold, live-region announcements, contrast, colour plus shape, and 44 px targets all landed.
- Theme toggle, tab switching, the D15 step mapping and reduced motion work.
