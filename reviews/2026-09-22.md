## Algorithm (paths)

Source tree: https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm at `main`, read 2026-09-22. `home-mixer/params/param.rs` header: mirrored from feature-switch defaults, last sync `2026-09-21T16:23:47Z`. README says those defaults are the primary production values, and that experiments at a notable share of traffic are meant to show up as diffs. `WeightPerturbationSigma` default is `0.0`.

Weights multiply predicted probabilities or continuous predictions. They do not multiply raw counts. Stated in `README.md` (14 Aug 2026) and in comments in `home-mixer/scorers/ranking_scorer.rs`. A 2023 engineering-blog weight is directional. These files do not print those 2023 numbers. There is no bookmark weight in `param.rs`.

**Positive defaults in `param.rs`.** Copy-link 20. Reply 5. Quote 5. DM-share 5. Follow-author 4. Share 2. Repost 1. Like 0.5. Click 0.4. Open-link 0.2. Video-open 0.07. Photo-expand 0.05. Quoted-click 0.05. Dwell 0.05. Post-unexplored 0.02, in-network only, additive (`EnableMultiplicativePostUnexplored` false). Continuous dwell time 0.004. Profile-click, video-quality-view, quoted video-quality-view, click-dwell, and active-seconds are 0.

**Negative defaults.** Report −234. Mute −58.8. Not interested −43.2. Block −31.2. Not-dwelled −0.02. The same file's comment: a report's baseline probability is far below a like, so the large weight is not "one report cancels 468 likes." Coordinated reports off-timeline do not score.

**Mutual-follow boost.** `docs/BIDIRECTIONAL_BOOST_CHANGE.md`: reply boost settled at 15 on 24 Jul 2026 after a 20 test. Current default `BidirectionalFollowReplyWeightBoost` is 15. `BidirectionalFollowDwellWeightBoost` is 0. In `ranking_scorer.rs`, `bidirectional_boost_eligible` requires an original post (no `in_reply_to_tweet_id`, no `retweeted_tweet_id`) and `is_mutual_follow_author == Some(true)`. A "hi" reply does not receive it. Hydration default is on.

**After the weighted sum, same scorer.** Author diversity is on: decay 0.5, floor 0.25, so later posts by the same author in one candidate pool are multiplied down toward 0.25. Out-of-network posts are multiplied by 0.75. `EnableOonRescoreForInNetworkRepliesRetweets` is true, so a follower's reply or repost is also multiplied by 0.75. README pre-score filter `OONRetweetReplyFilter` removes replies and reposts from accounts the viewer does not follow. For a stranger, the root is the post For You can rank. The cards are not.

**Age and cold start, `param.rs` plus README.** README `AgeFilter`: posts older than 48 hours. `SimclustersMaxCandidateAgeHours` 48. Cold start: follower cap 1000, impression threshold 1000, max post age 172800 seconds, slot 15 through 16, viewer cold-start boost on, Thompson sampling off. `PhoenixColdStartMaxResults` is 0. `ranking_scorer.rs` calls `AuthorColdStart`; the lift arithmetic lives in that other file, which this review did not open. Do not read "under 1,000 followers" as a promise of top-of-feed distribution. The named slot is 15–16.

**`grox/flows/reply_spam/`.** Four plans: `spam_comment`, `reply_ranking`, `multi_step_reply_spam`, `coordinated_spam`. Prompts are Jinja templates and are not in the tree (`prompts.py` says so, matching the README note that Grox prompts are withheld). The code enforces a score from 0 to 3 and nothing about what the prose rubric calls spam.

`task_filter.py` `TaskSpamFilter`: skips non-replies, skips a reply by the thread's own author, and skips when the parent or the root has more than `GROK_GEMMA_FOLLOWER_SPLIT` (200,000) followers. Those large-account replies go to reply ranking instead. His own card-to-card replies are not spam-scored. Replies other people leave him, and replies he leaves other people under 200k followers, are eligible. `task_write.py`: score `0.0` applies `SafetyLabelType.RiskyHighVizReply` unless the author is high page-rank v2, grey-badge, or a test user, and stores the score only when it is lower than an existing one. Coordinated spam (`plan_coordinated_spam.py`, filter threshold 80,000 followers in `TaskCoordinatedSpamFilter`) can write score 0 and the same label onto listed reply ids. This directory ranks and labels replies. It does not, in these files, change `RankingScorer`.

## Adversarial review

**Small N.** 33 followers and about 100 views on a settings hook, with 1 like, cannot pick a winner. The pin's 634 views and 25 replies prove that asking for "hi" gets "hi." They do not prove a format. The camera's one success reply is from @awakenluke, whose profile name is Luke Mckenzie. Treat it as the operator's other account until a stranger replies. Two bookmarks on Wi-Fi is not a signal the scorer uses.

**Repo defaults are not every request.** The header says the numbers were copied from production feature switches on 21 Sep 2026, and the README says a cron keeps them aligned. Experiments exist. Perturbation is off only at the default sigma of 0. A viewer's weights can differ without this review being able to see it. The 2023 blog is not in the files that were opened. Anything quoted from it stays directional.

**`reply_spam` can be over-read.** The rubric text is withheld. A score of 0 writes a reply label and a reply-ranking score. These files do not show that label changing For You rank. His own thread cards are skipped as same-user replies, so the spam flow is not what collapsed the 18 Pro closer from 70 views to 2. Telling him to stop "hi" replies is still right because the code path is real and the posts are bad. Telling him the pin already shadowbanned the account is not supported.

**Thread fatigue is only half the drop.** Author diversity applies when several of his posts sit in one candidate pool. The out-of-network reply filter may remove the cards before that decay runs. The camera counts (104, then ~40, then ~10–20) look like people opening the thread and leaving, which For You weights do not have to explain. A 9-card thread can still be the right object on the Following tab. This repository is the For You mixer. It does not describe Following.

**Cold start is a slot, not a strategy.** Follower cap 1000 and impression cap 1000 include this account today. The published slot is positions 15–16, the extra Phoenix cold-start source cap is 0, and the lift function was not read. "Post more while you still qualify" does not follow. "Post nothing, to stay eligible" does not follow either. The hold on smart-TV is about that draft's hook and its generated image, not about hoarding impressions.

**What the repo cannot see.** Who the 13 follows are. Whether any viewer is a mutual, which is the only place the +15 reply boost exists. Premium long-post treatment. Notification delivery of thread replies. Under the Hood labels on this account. Whether the public "Made with AI" badge is a ranking feature. Australian time-of-day. The other ~70 posts. Copy-link is the heaviest positive weight and also an action this account has no evidence of earning. Optimizing the week for copy-link would be cosplay.

**The useful week is smaller than the algorithm.** One bio, one unpinned bait post, one pinned settings hook, replies only where a person named a result, and the smart-TV draft left in the folder until the first sentence and the photograph match the cards. The weights explain why the root has to carry the thread. They do not explain a growth plan for 33 followers.
