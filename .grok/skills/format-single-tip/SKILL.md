# Format: single-tip

One post. No replies from the account. It is eligible for the small-author lift that replies are not (`reference/x-algorithm.md` A5).

## Shape

1. **Result** — sentence 1 names the device and what changes.
2. **Why** — one sentence on why the default is wrong.
3. **Path** — the exact path from `PATHS.md`, or the exact action.
4. **Payoff** — what the reader notices next time.
5. **Skip line** — when the menu can be missing.

Under 280 characters when possible, so it fits a free account. Never over 600.

## Research and fact-check

- A setting tip: `/verify-settings <folder>` paths mode, `PATHS.md`.
- A non-setting claim: `/verify-settings <folder> claims`, `CLAIMS.md`.

## Images

Optional. A real screenshot of the setting beats an illustration. AI images only as labelled illustration.
