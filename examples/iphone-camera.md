<!-- thesis -->
The camera was never the problem. Training wheels still on: grid off, safest style, resolution wound down, choices wiped on close. Setup screens were never going to tell you.

<!-- promise -->
🧵 Here are the 7 settings that fixed it:

---

<!-- card 1: title / default / path / action / payoff -->
**Grid and Level**
Blank viewfinder. Subject dead-center. Horizon a few degrees off.
Settings → Camera. Grid on. Level on.
Face, plate, or skyline on a line. Wait for yellow.
Composition, not hardware.

<!-- card 2 -->
**AE/AF Lock**
One tap and the phone keeps guessing. Face goes soft. Window blows out.
Tap and hold until AE/AF LOCK. Slide exposure.
Same room. Same second. Face stays sharp.

<!-- card 3 -->
**Photographic Styles**
Standard: safe, flat. A style is processing while you shoot, not a filter on top.
Camera → Styles, or Settings → Camera → Photographic Styles. Pick one. Leave it.
Colour decided in the room, not later.

<!-- card 4 -->
**Resolution**
Sensor can do 24MP or 48MP. Default is lower to save storage.
Settings → Camera → Formats. Photo Mode 24MP daily. ProRAW & Resolution Control, then 48MP, when you crop.
<!-- skip -->
No 24/48 toggle? Skip it. Grid, lock, and Styles still apply.

<!-- card 5 -->
**Default focal length**
Pro opens ~24mm. Whole table. Faces stretch. Exit sign in the edge.
Settings → Camera → Main Camera (some say Fusion Camera). Extra lengths on. Default 28mm or 35mm.
<!-- skip -->
No Main Camera menu? Skip it. Use 1x. Take one step forward.

<!-- card 6 -->
**Volume button as the shutter**
On-screen shutter shoves the phone at the moment it needs to be still.
Volume Up is shutter. Hold for burst. Index finger on the button. Elbows in.
One frame nobody blinks.

<!-- card 7 -->
**Preserve Settings**
Close Camera and the first six die. Factory setup returns.
Settings → Camera → Preserve Settings. On last, so 1–6 stick.
Camera opens the way you left it.
