## Hook lines that are weak

## Rules promoted

Three, in `.grok/skills/hidden-settings/SKILL.md`. Settings threads keep the seven beats, `Setting N`, and the one-line recap.

1. Comparison hook. Both products, one question, each match on its own `✓` line, a count only, then `🧵` alone.
2. Difference card. `N. Plain name`, a product term glossed on that line, then what a person notices, then the sourced spec. A path only when `PATHS.md` has a control. One difference per post. Numbers match the count.
3. Comparison closer. `The full pass`, then one `✓` line per difference, written `name = payoff`, using that card's figure and model.

Not promoted: `@apple`, "Matching up", "latest", "You decide if it's worth upgrading", `💬`, "Reply with your thoughts on this years release", the VERIFY lines, the duplicate 3, "It is a phone sensor", "not a new button", "One afternoon on one phone is not the rating", the loosened closer figures, and the Newsroom plates.
