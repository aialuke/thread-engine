# REPLIES: talking points for post 1

For replies under post 1, which is still live. These are points to make, not text to paste: write each reply in your own words, in one or two sentences, answering the row the person raised. Never edit the post to add a caveat. Sources are in `CLAIMS.md`.

- **"Photopea has ads."** Yes: the free version shows an ad beside the canvas, and Premium ($5 a month) is how you support it. It's free, not open source.
- **"Resolve isn't free, it's Studio."** The free version edits, grades and mixes up to UHD 60 fps; Studio ($295) adds the AI tools, extra effects and noise reduction. Once you've tested it: normal exports from the free version carry no watermark. Studio-only effects are the ones that show one.
- **"Blender isn't After Effects."** True. After Effects composites layers on a 2D timeline, and Blender is a 3D scene. It covers a lot of motion work, differently.
- **"Streamlabs is free."** Streamlabs Desktop is. Ultra, from $15.75 a month, is the paid layer (multistreaming, themes, apps), and OBS is what people move to when they drop it.
- **"OBS can't edit."** Right, it records and streams. Resolve is the editor in this list.
- **"Krita isn't on iPad."** Right: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and ChromeOS. Krita's own page says iPads aren't supported, so it isn't Procreate on the iPad.
