# Audience promise

Provisional. The operator can change it; the weekly review reports whether posts keep to it.

**Promise:** useful tech that saves an Australian creator or device owner money or time.

## Main lane

A post is in the `main` lane when all three are true:

1. A reader can act on it today with something they already own or can get free.
2. The payoff is money or time saved, or a purchase avoided.
3. The subject is a device, an app, a setting, or a creator tool.

Settings threads, product comparisons that change a buying decision, paid-to-free tool swaps, and single tips all fit.

## Other lane

Everything else is `other`: building in public, AI industry commentary, networking posts, opinions with no action.

`other` posts are allowed. The algorithm reference (fact A8) measures topic share over the last 15 originals. Keep at least 12 of the last 15 originals in `main`. That 80 percent is an editorial choice, not a proven algorithm threshold.
